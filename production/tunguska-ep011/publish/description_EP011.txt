The Explosion That Left No Crater (Tunguska 1908)

On June 30, 1908, a powerful explosion over central Siberia flattened about 2,000 square kilometers of forest, yet no giant impact crater was found. Using NASA Landsat imagery from 2024, a photograph from Leonid Kulik's 1929 expedition and labelled reconstructions, we look at the evidence for an airburst: a cosmic object releasing its energy in the air, kilometers above the ground.

No camera recorded the 1908 event. Every moving image of the explosion in this video is a reconstruction, not footage. Energy and size figures are model-dependent estimates. The Chelyabinsk 2013 sequence is a schematic, not footage of Tunguska.
