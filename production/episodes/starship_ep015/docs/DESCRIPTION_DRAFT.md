## Description

On November 14, 2026, SpaceX's tower caught a Starship upper stage after it came back from orbit, a first. On the same flight the Super Heavy booster was lost. Here is what actually happened on Flight 15, why catching a spacecraft is so hard, and what still has to be proven.

Facts as of November 15, 2026. The cause of the booster abort has not been confirmed.
