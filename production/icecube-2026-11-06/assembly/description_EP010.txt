TITLE: Why Scientists Buried a Telescope Under Antarctica

Beneath the South Pole, the IceCube Neutrino Observatory uses Antarctic ice to detect particles from space. We explore the original deep array, how hot-water drilling made its construction possible, and how a rare flash of light can begin a search for a distant source.

This episode distinguishes the original array, completed in 2010, from the upgrade deployed in 2026. Detector graphics are explanatory schematics. Space animations are visualizations. Antarctic traverse footage is contextual field footage, not a record of IceCube's construction.
