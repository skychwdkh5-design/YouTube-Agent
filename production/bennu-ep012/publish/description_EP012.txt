TITLE: NASA Touched an Asteroid. Then It Started Sinking.

On October 20, 2020, NASA's OSIRIS-REx spacecraft reached down to asteroid Bennu to grab a sample. The surface barely pushed back: the sampler arm sank nearly half a meter (about 1.5 ft), and it was still sinking when the back-away thrusters fired. Here is the contact second by second, why Bennu's surface behaved like a ball pit, the mark it left behind, the leaking sampler head, and the 121.6 grams that made it back to Earth.
