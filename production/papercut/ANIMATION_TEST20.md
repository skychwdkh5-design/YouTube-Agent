- New: `assets/sheet_cutouts/` — 16 drawings + 1 effect cut 1:1 from the approved sheet by
  `tools/extract_sheet.py` (flood-fill background, keep the largest figure, add a ring of the sheet's

## Implemented and visually checked
| Motion | How | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Pose switching | hard cut between approved drawings, feet line kept, 4-frame paper "pop" (scale 0.97→1.0) | works: hero → point → hero → telescope → surprised → hero |
| Blink | 3 phases (open → half lid → closed → half → open, 5 frames). Open = untouched hero pixels; half/closed = skin patch + ink stroke in the hero's own ink colour | works on hero |
| Mouth flap ("lip sync" by loudness) | 3 states driven by voice loudness, min hold 2 frames: open (original), mid (original mouth squashed 55 %), closed (skin + smile stroke) | works on hero; NOT phoneme lip sync |
| Reaction effect | "!!" cut from the sheet pops beside the head | works |
| Interaction with graphics | pointing pose at the blocked line; telescope pose with sight lines into a view of real NASA footage; surprised pose on NASA's impact-flash animation | works |
| Whole-figure motion | presets from MOTION_LIBRARY (bob / roll / scale within QA gates) | works |

## Missing — needs new approved art (not generated by us)
1. **Arms / hands animation**: separate glove, forearm and upper-arm pieces with painted overlap at the
   elbow and wrist, for the hero pose (both arms), plus the hidden part of the helmet behind the raised glove.
2. **Legs / walking**: separate legs with hidden hip and knee geometry. Not in the pack.
3. **Phoneme mouths for the hero**: at least 6–8 mouth shapes (closed M/B/P, E, A, O, U, F/V, L) drawn
   on the hero's face at hero scale. Now we have only open (original) plus two derived states.
4. **Eye directions / brows** for the hero (look left/right/up), so he can follow objects.
5. **High-resolution poses and heads**: the sheet poses are only ~200 px tall (the hero is 733 px).
   On screen they are upscaled ~1.6x and look softer than the hero. A ≥ 700 px version of each pose
   would remove the difference.
6. **Expressions on the hero body**: the six emotion heads are a different size and angle than the
   hero's head, so they cannot be swapped onto the hero body without changing the look. They can
   be used only as separate inserts.
7. **Transition drawings** (in-betweens) between poses, if cuts should become smooth moves.
