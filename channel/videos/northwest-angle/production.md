## Hard visual rules for the edit (from the ledger)

1. **Mitchell map (V1, V2):** no arrows, labels, circles or highlights on Lake of the Woods or the upper
   Mississippi. Crops stay on the full sheet, the cartouche and the general interior. Never place
   Mitchell's lake or river next to modern geography for comparison (C5, C6 are UNVERIFIED).
2. **Canoe route (Shots 074-075):** drawn as a schematic line, labelled "(schematic)". Its real course
   is not in the ledger.
3. **"Several northwestern points" (Shot 070-071):** do NOT place unnamed candidate markers on the map.
   Only Rat Portage and Angle Inlet get map positions.
4. **Jim's Corner:** a generic booth icon with a text label only - no map pin, no photo (location not in
   the ledger; all known photos are rejected in visuals.md).
5. **Ice road (Shots 096-100):** a schematic arrow, labelled "schematic". No endpoints, no resort or town
   names, no fee, no operator, no year, nothing that says it runs this winter. The winter Landsat scene
   is labelled "Winter (Landsat)" only; its acquisition date goes in the credits, not on screen.
6. **Boat route (Shot 094):** schematic arrow, no harbors or operators named.
7. **No CBP, CBSA, TIME, or tourism-bureau logos or photos** anywhere (rejected in visuals.md).
8. **Every on-screen number** is in the On-screen text list (section D) with its ledger ID. Nothing else
   goes on screen.
