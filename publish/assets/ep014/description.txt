NASA Astronauts Saw Five Flashes on the Far Side of the Moon

During the Artemis II lunar flyby, Orion passed behind the Moon and lost radio contact with Earth, as planned. While Mission Control waited, the crew watched the dark lunar surface and marked where they saw brief pinpricks of light, likely meteoroid impact flashes. Here is where Orion was, what the crew could see, what an impact flash is, and how their color-coded notes became evidence for NASA's lunar science team.
