NASA Lost Contact With Voyager 1. Then the Signal Came Back.

In October 2026, NASA's Deep Space Network listened for Voyager 1 two days in a row and heard nothing. Before engineers sent a troubleshooting command, the signal came back on its own. Here is what happened, what the spacecraft had been doing, and why every question to Voyager now takes two days to answer: on November 18, 2026, Voyager 1 is about one light-day from Earth.
