## Measured
- Hand-written by the agent: `research/notes.json` 10.8 KB, `script.md` 5.5 KB, `qa/visual_review.json` 4.7 KB, topic approval 0.2 KB = **21 KB**. Generated by code from them plus free satellite data: claims.json, report, brief, episode.json, graphics_spec.json, 8 graphics, storyboard, timeline, labels, qa report, REVIEW.md, voice plan = **177 KB of JSON/Markdown and ~10 MB of images**, with no hand-written JSON beyond notes.
- Before this round the agent also hand-wrote claims.json, episode.json and graphics_spec.json (about 25 KB of structured JSON). That work is now derived or generated.
- Of 14 stages: 9 FULLY_AUTOMATED (facts, evidence, visualplan, visuals, storyboard, captions, qc, release + the checking half of visualcheck), 4 need the agent's intellectual work (topic, research, script, visual review), 2 need the owner (voice, render).
- The agent's work is still real work: finding and reading sources, deciding what the evidence supports, writing the script, looking at frames. It is validated and made resumable, not replaced.

## What is NOT automated and why
1. Searching and reading sources (needs an LLM; primaries are blocked by the network policy, so confidence is capped at medium).
2. The prose of the script.
3. Judging what a picture shows when no measurement exists (honest NEEDS_VISUAL_REVIEW).
4. Owner decisions: paid voice, full render, publishing, main.
5. Not yet exercised: the real ElevenLabs call and the full render inside the new chain (stubbed / plan-only); topic discovery end to end on a new topic (unit-tested and live `collect` verified, not run through the orchestrator on a fresh subject).
