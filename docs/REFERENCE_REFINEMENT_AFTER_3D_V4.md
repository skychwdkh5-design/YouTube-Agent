# Reference refinement after 3D v4

2026-11-01. Owner asks to revisit uploaded reference and identify further improvements. Source: uploaded 37.945s screen recording; observations below are visual, with approximate source timestamps. No new audio transcription or measured retention claim.

## Diagnosis (editorial assessment)
V4 changes camera angle many times but repeatedly presents the same semantic event: wheel -> rock -> crystals. The reference changes narrative state: departure -> travel -> meeting -> larger offer -> surprised reaction -> rewind -> deceptive peace -> expansion plan -> economic mechanism. Number of cuts or 3D geometry alone is not the objective. Prioritize new meaning, expressive staging and transitions; improve artwork/material quality as a separate requirement.

## Additional transferable techniques
1. Outcome-first cold open, then rewind for causal explanation (reference 0–14s). Our next pilot should show a striking detail first, create one concrete question, then rewind to reveal its cause. Rewind must serve story order, not demonstrate an effect.
2. Expectation vs result with visual contrast: one city vs whole territory and shocked faces (6–12s). Depict a scale/role difference in the same composition when verified; never invent scientific comparison values.
3. Two meanings in one shot: handshake plus hidden knives (18–20s). A second object/gesture adds irony or motive. Transfer the narrative principle rather than literal knives/humans to unrelated science topics.
4. Nested scene transition: expansion map becomes Napoleon's thought bubble (25–27s). Turn the current visual into an object in the next scene: camera image, monitor, microscope view, map inset, or framed hypothesis. Do not label unsupported imagined scientific processes as established facts.
5. Props replace abstract nouns: island wealth becomes money, then sugar/coffee icons (34–38s). Use clear original 3D props and concise overlays where useful, not generic grids or long paragraphs.
6. Consistent visual anchors: map palette, recurring character silhouettes and locations help maintain continuity across moves. Preserve one recognizable anchor through transitions.
7. Readable, intentionally nonliteral scale: characters and props are oversized against map; this is illustrative staging, not measurement. In documentary diagrams distinguish metaphorical scale from quantitative comparisons.
8. Sequential attention: territorial color -> yellow oval/label -> outward arrows -> thought bubble (23–27s). Introduce one dominant cue, clear or demote it before the next; avoid stacking all layers at once.
9. Expressive secondary action: faces/hand gestures/knife sparkle add reaction and meaning. For machinery use believable stop, mast turn, instrument movement, delayed debris/settling rather than forced cartoon eyes.
10. Alternate short reveals and readable holds; vary motion speed around impact/reveal. These are proposed production choices, not precise timing/easing measurements from source code.

## Art correction
V4 is a procedural low-detail 3D motion test; do not treat crude faceting, flat materials or simplified contact as a final visual reference. Need better silhouettes, physically readable contact, surface texture, lighting hierarchy and polished composition. Owner requirement is beyond flat 2D; it does not automatically require that every map, caption, line or source photo be rebuilt as 3D. Proposed approach is detailed 3D action plus verified imagery, maps and integrated motion graphics. Do not claim the owner approved this hybrid look yet.

## Revised acceptance
Each shot must have a new narrative function (question, action, consequence, comparison, evidence, reaction or answer). A new angle without new meaning does not count as a new story beat. Design one strong discovery/reveal, one purposeful scene bridge, one expressive secondary action and one actual evidence reveal. Avoid replaying one action from many angles solely to fill duration. On-screen copy should communicate a specific curiosity, not placeholders such as 'a surprise inside'. Scripts require verified sources before making factual claims.

This is a planning refinement, not another rendered pilot, approved style or production-renderer upgrade. Existing fact, rights, cost and publication gates remain.
