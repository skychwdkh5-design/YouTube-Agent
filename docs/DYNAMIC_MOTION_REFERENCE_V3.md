## Core correction
The desired approach is narrative 2D animation with staged object actions, not cinematic grading plus pan/zoom on a still. Characters, props, map masks, reactions and camera choreography explain each meaningful story beat. Do not require a literal new image for every grammatical word. Visualize semantic units and their actions.

## Production requirements
Write storyboard as narration beat -> actor/object -> action -> visual result -> camera move -> next-scene bridge. Build independent transformable layers and a reusable puppet/prop library. Animate objects and consequences before adding camera movement. Transitions should carry story meaning: travel follows a route, rewind reverses previous states, thought bubble nests and reveals a scene. Use foreground/subject/background hierarchy and readable contrast. Retain factual and rights checks; political borders require verified period geometry. Do not copy the reference artwork or imply artistic scenes are documentary footage.
