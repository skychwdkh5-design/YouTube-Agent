# Thumbnail playbook

Read before the three concepts of every new long episode. Works together with the Thumbnail-Hook
rule (CLAUDE.md, yt-qc `thumbnail_hook_*`) and docs/THUMBNAIL_STYLE_GUIDE.md (channel style: large uppercase headline, white/yellow emphasis, dramatic satellite/aerial imagery).

## Every cover
- Readable on a phone: test at 320 px wide.
- **One** main visual object. If you need two, the idea is not finished.
- Minimum text: 4-5 words at most, uppercase, never a copy of the title (different words, same promise).
- Raises one **concrete** question the video answers ("why won't it go to the Moon?"), not "shocking".
- Promises nothing the video does not show. The cover object is on screen in the first 3 seconds.
- Reconstructions are labelled in the video; a cover never presents an invented event as a photo.

## Three concepts, one choice
Write three title + cover + first-15-s packages into `thumbnail_hook.json`. Choose by: honesty of the
promise, whether the cover object exists as real legal footage for second 0, strength of the question.
A CTR guess is a hypothesis, never the reason stated as fact. Keep the two alternates for A/B.
