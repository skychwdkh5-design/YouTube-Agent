## Camera behaviour (observed)
Continuous camera, eased; pans 0.5–1.5 s, zoom-ins 0.5–0.8 s with blur, holds ≤ 2 s. Subjects (shapes, icons) stay registered to the map under zoom. No visible 3D tilt after the first seconds.

## Inference `[inferred]`
Compositing with a flat map texture + vector overlays, icons as sprites with pop/overshoot, trails as glowing lines with a bright head, burned-in text. Unknown tool.
