## Design principles read from it
1. One idea per screen; type is the hero, the grid is the stage. 2. Contrast of weight: thin outline vs extra-bold in the same lockup. 3. Words arrive on beat, not all at once; emphasised words change colour or size. 4. A light cue (sweep, line head, glow) precedes or accompanies the reveal. 5. Impact moments are short and rare (colour cards, big duplicates); everything else is calm. 6. One accent colour. 7. Nothing is static for long: drift, glow pulse, scrolling grid.

## Not reproduced / uncertain
Outline-only type, full-frame colour card, axis-with-ticks, timecode counter, noise glitch (deliberately not built: it distracts in documentary), ring loader. The purple/magenta palette is a **design example**: OrbitalAtlas keeps its own tokens (amber emphasis, cyan data, violet only as a rare decor accent). No audio analysed.
