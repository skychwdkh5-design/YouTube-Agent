# EP003 - draft script, hook and first act (NOT LOCKED: for the visual proof; every sentence maps to a verified claim in story/claims.json)

[VIS: label="GREAT SALT LAKE, UTAH" claims=C1,C6]
This is the Great Salt Lake in Utah, the largest saline lake in the Western Hemisphere. In June 1985, its surface measured 4,209.7 feet.

[VIS: claims=C1,C2]
In July 2022, it measured 4,190.2 feet. The lake's record high had come in 1986, at 4,211.6 feet. Now it sat more than 20 feet lower.

[VIS: asset=gsl_timelapse claims=C6]
NASA's Landsat satellites were watching the whole time. Put the pictures in order, from 1984 to 2023, and the lake pulls back from its shore.

[VIS: claims=C3,C4]
Why? NASA's account points to people and to climate. For about a century, water has been diverted upstream for homes and farms. Utah State University researchers estimate that inflows have fallen 39 percent since 1850.

[VIS: claims=C5]
And the region kept growing. The Salt Lake City metro area went from 700,000 people in 1982 to 1.2 million in 2022.
