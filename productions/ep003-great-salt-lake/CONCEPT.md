# EP003 concept - the Great Salt Lake from orbit

Status: DRAFT. Chosen by `tools/autopilot/topics.py` (see `research/topic_decision.json`). Nothing here is locked; nothing was voiced.

## Angle
Not "the lake is dying" (heavily covered, including a 2026 Sundance feature) but **what forty years of Landsat measure, and what they cannot**:
the gauge numbers (4,209.7 ft in June 1985; 4,190.2 ft in July 2022) next to the same place seen from orbit, then the causes NASA names
(diversion for homes and farms, a warming climate, a population that grew from 700,000 to 1.2 million), each stated with its evidence type.
Documentary stance: show the evidence, say what is estimate vs observation, never promise an outcome.

## Audience (CLAUDE.md)
US first: a US landmark in the news (Utah), plain-English, US units with the metre equivalent only when the page itself is consistent
(the NASA page's metre figure for July 2022 is wrong - feet only). No Scandinavian angle is forced.

## Structure (target 8-10 min)
1. **Hook (0-60 s)** the two gauge readings and the record high, over the 1985 and 2022-23 Landsat frames. *Draft script exists (script.md).*
2. **Forty years in thirteen seconds** the NASA SVS time series (1984-2023) as a continuous sequence, then year-by-year stills.
3. **Why** diversion, climate, inflow -39 % since 1850 (Utah State, estimate), population. *Draft script exists.*
4. **What the exposed lake bed means** TO RESEARCH (no claim is allowed until read in a primary source): dust, wildlife, the causeway, salinity.
5. **What is being done / what the data says next** TO RESEARCH (state water policy, USGS/Utah agencies). No forecast without a cited model.
6. **How to read these pictures** method note: Landsat natural colour, what changes with season and sensor, why the lake looks different colours.
