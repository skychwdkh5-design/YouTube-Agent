## Central question
What is happening beneath Jakarta, how can satellites measure it - and what can't they see?

## Three title + hook combinations (hook scores are the repo's heuristic `hookscore.py`; it is tuned for short-form "you" hooks - not evidence of audience success)
| | title | spoken hook | evidence behind it | hookscore |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (opening visual) | Jakarta Is Sinking - Satellites Reveal Why | "Every red patch on this map is ground that is dropping - in places, almost five inches in a single year - and radar satellites caught it from orbit." | C01-C03 (ESA 2017-18, LOS, ~12 cm/yr local) | 33 WEAK |
| B (measurement mystery) | The Part of Jakarta Satellites Can't See | "A satellite hundreds of miles up can feel the ground drop by a few millimeters. So why can't it see part of Jakarta's coast?" | C09 (north coast masked, coherence < 0.6) | 36 WEAK |
| C (cause) | Jakarta Is Sinking - One Water Well at a Time | "Nobody is pushing Jakarta down. The ground is collapsing from underneath, one water well at a time - and you can watch it happen from space." | ESA caption, reviews (SNIPPET), NASA EO | 39 WEAK |

**Selected: A for the title/first frame, B as the mid-video open loop.** Reasons: A uses the strongest authentic image (R1/R5) and a dated, sourced number; B is the genuinely
surprising finding in the primary source and pays off in Scene 5; C is a plausible but simpler causal sentence and is left to the Scene 3 payoff.
Rejected alternatives from the brief: "The City Sinking Beneath Its Own Weight?" (not defensible - construction load is one minor factor, groundwater is not "its own weight");
"Why Parts of Jakarta Are Sinking Into the Sea" (implies the sea is the cause). Title stays "Jakarta Is Sinking - Satellites Reveal Why".

## Opening (0-30 s) as designed - every line backed by the matrix
- 0-5 s: first frame = measured ESA map (dated stamp "SENTINEL-1 RADAR - 2017-2018 - LINE OF SIGHT"). VO: "Every red patch on this map is ground that is sinking - in places almost five inches in a single year." [C01]
- 5-15 s: VO: "Radar satellites measured it from orbit. And what they show isn't one city sinking - it's several bowls, falling at different speeds." (bowl count NOT stated until C21 read; Data in Brief supports "three hotspots", C06). Visual: the map's three clusters pulse in turn.
- 15-30 s: VO: "Why is the ground falling? Why has one of those bowls slowed down? And why can't the same satellites see part of the coast?" Visual: hard cut to the Earth->Java->Jakarta geographic transition (Seq. A).
Avoided: "entirely underwater by 2050", any current-rate claim, apocalyptic framing.
