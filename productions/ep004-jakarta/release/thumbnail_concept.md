Title pairing: "Jakarta Is Sinking – Satellites Reveal Why". The thumbnail must not repeat the title: it adds the tension "what radar can't see".
- Picture: our own real Landsat 8 frame (14 Sep 2026) of Jakarta Bay and the coastline, darkened on the left for text. No ESA frame, no stock, no AI imagery.
- Text (max 5 words, 1 line = 1 idea): "JAKARTA / IS SINKING" (white + amber, ~60 % of frame height) and a small chip "WHAT RADAR CAN'T SEE" (cyan bar), echoing the video's closing question.
- Credit chip bottom-left "LANDSAT 8 · 14 SEP 2026 · NASA/USGS" (keeps the evidence honest; remove if it clutters at 160 px width).
- Do NOT put a rate (e.g. "12 cm") as the headline: it is a peak at individual spots, line of sight, and a bare number on a thumbnail would mislead.
- Mobile check: headline readable at 160x90 px; contrast white/amber on the dark gradient; one focal area (the bay), no small text except the credit.
- A/B variant: replace the chip with "5 INCHES A YEAR*" only if the owner accepts a footnote-style qualifier; default stays the safer version above.
