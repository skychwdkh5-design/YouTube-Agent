## Central question
What is happening beneath Jakarta, how can satellites measure it, can it be slowed?

## What the snippets say (each needs primary confirmation)
1. **Rates differ widely by study, period and footprint - there is no single "Jakarta rate".**
   - Abidin et al. review (PIAHS 372, 2015): leveling/GPS/InSAR 1974-2010, typical 3-10 cm/yr, spatially and temporally variable; some sites 20-28 cm/yr (1982-2010 set).
   - Chaussard, Amelung, Abidin (ALOS, 2006-2009): up to 24 cm/yr across six Indonesian cities - Jakarta-specific maximum unconfirmed.
   - ESA image (Sentinel-1, 2017-2018): local sinking up to ~12 cm/yr (snippet of the ESA page; map extent/legend unread).
   - EGU21-14347 abstract (Sentinel-1A, 2019-2020): province rates +1.8 to -10.7 cm/yr; North Jakarta mean ~-4.9 cm/yr.
   - Harintaka et al., Geocarto Int. 2024 (multi-track SBAS, 2017-2022): largest subsidence Cikarang and coastal North Jakarta; one reading says a surge
     around mid-2018 then deceleration by 2022, another abstract says "linear to this day" -> **disagreement, unresolved**.
2. **Causes (review level):** groundwater extraction, load of construction on compressible young alluvium, natural consolidation (Abidin 2015). Rates correlate
   with abstraction volumes at several sites. Not the same thing as sea-level rise or climate change.
3. **Policy (news-level, not science):** Governor Reg. 93/2021 groundwater-free zones (in force 1 Aug 2023, enforcement criticised); Pergub 5/2026 (Feb 2026) tightens
   groundwater use in buildings; PAM Jaya piped coverage reported variously 62 % / ~80 % / 81 % (definitions unclear). Jakarta Post/ANTARA - dates and wording unread.
4. **Sea wall (news-level):** governor said (Jan 2026) groundbreaking targeted for Sept 2026, awaiting central government; Jakarta share 12 -> 19 km; NCICD sections
   partly built (Ancol 1.2 km, Muara Angke 1.1 km, Pluit 400/600 m by 2025). **Whether the Sept 2026 groundbreaking happened is UNVERIFIED.**
5. **Recovery / uplift (the planned twist):** no search result confirmed localized uplift or recovery. The 2026 RSASE paper is the lead; unread. Do NOT script
   a "twist" until read. A deceleration claim has a source (Harintaka 2024) but a conflicting abstract.
