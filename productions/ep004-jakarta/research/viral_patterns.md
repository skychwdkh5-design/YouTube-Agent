# Viral / performance patterns and EP004 rules (2026-10-15)

Basis: titles, durations, view counts and thumbnails of the 19 videos in `competitor_analysis.md`. No transcripts or video were available, so these are
**packaging and format patterns**, not pacing or retention findings. Public views are observed performance, not proof of what caused it.

## Observed patterns
1. **One geographic object, one idea** (14/19 thumbnails): a map, aerial or render with 0-5 words. Arrow/circle on the focus in 9/19.
2. **Title = a mechanism or paradox, not a statistic**: "Why Jakarta is sinking", "Why Nobody Lives on...", "Why Planes Avoid...", "...Only Kinda Works".
   When a number is used it is a single comparison ("75x Faster Than Venice", "8.2 METRES AND STILL SINKING").
3. **A scale anchor the viewer already owns**: fern overlays New York on the Netherlands; Edinger shows steps that "used to be street level".
4. **The hidden mechanism made physical**: Practical Engineering's see-through sand tank (14.3 M views); Half as Interesting's cut-away of a city over an aquifer.
5. **Authentic evidence frame in the thumbnail** (BBC mosque, Vox flood) - high emotion without composites.
6. Length: the set spans 6:37-27:08. Short-explainer examples sit at 6:37-6:51 (BBC, Vox China, HAI) - our 7-9 min target is inside the observed range.

## Five practical rules for EP004 and what each changes in the storyboard
| # | rule | how it changes the storyboard |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Thumbnail, title and first frame show the same single object**: the measured ground-motion map with one callout | Scene 1's first frame is the measured map (ESA/CC-licensed, dated), not a logo or a globe. Thumbnail concept A uses the same map crop. |
| R2 | **Title asks "why/how we know", not "how fast"**; the number appears as a dated, located measurement | No title or hook leads with "X cm/yr" alone; every number carries period + place + "line-of-sight" where applicable (claim matrix). |
| R3 | **Give each key number a physical anchor** | 12 cm/yr -> a hand-width/inches overlay drawn next to a street-level scale figure (illustration class); the cross-section scene carries a scale bar and an explicit "not to scale" disclaimer. |
| R4 | **Show the hidden mechanism as an explorable cut-away, not narration over maps** | Sequence C is a layered 2.5D cross-section with water level dropping, clay layers compacting; it is on screen for the entire cause explanation (no map under a voice explaining pumping). |
| R5 | **Authentic proof over decoration**: use real measured/dated imagery and avoid composites; keep illustrations visibly labelled | Radar-geometry animation is stamped ILLUSTRATION and followed by a hard cut to the measured map; no fake flood composites; thumbnails use evidence crops only. |
