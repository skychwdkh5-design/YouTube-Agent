NASA's Curiosity rover drove over a rock on Mars. It cracked open, and this was inside.

Yellow crystals. Pure sulfur, the first ever found on Mars.

An instrument on the arm confirmed it: sulfur, on its own.

On Earth, pure sulfur forms near volcanoes and hot springs. There's no evidence of either here.

The mission's project scientist said it plainly: it shouldn't be there.

A new study dates it to about three billion years ago. How it formed is still debated.
