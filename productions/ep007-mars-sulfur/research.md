# EP007 — Yellow sulfur crystals on Mars (research, pre-script)

Status: facts verified 2026-10-28; images NOT downloaded (NASA hosts blocked in the cloud session).

## Confirmed (NASA/JPL)
- 2024-05-30, sol 4,200: Curiosity drove over and crushed a rock in Gediz Vallis channel, Mount Sharp, Gale crater; yellow crystals inside. [JPL 2024-100]
- APXS (arm) identified the material as elemental sulfur; first pure sulfur found on Mars. Rock nickname "Convict Lake" (MAHLI close-up, sol 4,205, 2024-06-04). [PIA26307]
- "Snow Lake" close-up 2024-06-08, sol 4,209. [PIA26308]
- A whole field of similar bright stones; too brittle to drill. Drill hole #41 in nearby rock "Mammoth Lakes", 2024-06-18. [JPL 2024-100]
- Why surprising: earlier sulfur on Mars was in sulfates/minerals; elemental sulfur forms only in a narrow range of conditions not linked to this site's history; on Earth tied to volcanoes/hot springs, no evidence of either on Mount Sharp. Vasavada: "It shouldn't be there, so now we have to explain it." [JPL 2024-100, 2024-160]
- HiRISE could not resolve the stones; from orbit the patch looked unremarkable. [JPL 2024-160]
- Last look 2024-10-11, then rover left for the boxwork. [JPL 2024-160]
- Channel: carved late, by floods, debris flows and dry avalanches. [JPL 2024-100, 2024-160]

## Peer-reviewed (2026) — present as "the team proposes"
- VanBommel et al., Science 393, 820–825 (Aug 20 2026), doi:10.1126/science.adu5501: stones are native sulfur; deposit "appears to have formed in place" in a canyon cut into the Gediz Vallis floor; proposed source magmatic vapor that cooled in the near-subsurface cryosphere, released as the valley eroded. WashU release: roughly 3 billion years old; "likely" water involved; no evidence microbes played a role.

## Competing hypothesis (conference abstracts, not peer reviewed)
- Rapin et al., Maggioni et al. (EGU26 / LPSC 2026): impact melted a sulfur-rich upstream unit; liquid sulfur flowed ~4 km and crystallized. Conditional on an unmeasured sulfur-rich yardangs unit.

## Do NOT say
- Evidence of life / biosignature. No video of the crushing exists — any reconstruction must be labelled.
- "Sulfur smell" (elemental sulfur is odorless per JPL).
