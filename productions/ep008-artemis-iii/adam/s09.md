First docking: Orion pulls up alongside the Blue Moon lander. While they're joined, Orion's own software flies both spacecraft. Then the hatch opens. Up to two astronauts, in their orange survival suits, float inside. NASA calls it its first evaluation of a commercial lunar lander with a crew. They spend about two days checking its cabin, controls and life support. Riding along is a dummy the weight of a spacesuit, wired with sensors, reporting what the cabin really feels like.
