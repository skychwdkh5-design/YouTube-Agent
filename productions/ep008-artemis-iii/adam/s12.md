So why spend a whole mission on this? NASA's Artemis program manager put it plainly: every piece of the mission in Earth orbit is designed to reduce risk before astronauts go back to the Moon. Three spacecraft on three different rockets, working together, which he says NASA has never attempted. And on Artemis Four, the landing depends on exactly this step. Orion can't land. A commercial lander has to meet it, take astronauts down near the South Pole, and bring them back. So the first test of this handoff happens close to home, in Earth orbit.
