## How we looked
19. So what can a satellite add? Not opinions. Just pixels.
20. We used free Sentinel-2 images from the European Copernicus program.
21. Each pixel in our frames covers 10 meters of ground.
22. That is sharp enough to see a long cleared strip or a road, but not a person, a crane, or a window.
23. We picked seven scenes from the same orbit, taken in late September or October of each year from 2020 to 2026.
24. Same orbit means the satellite looks from the same angle at the same time of day, so hills and roads line up from year to year.
25. Same season means the light and the sand look alike, so a change is more likely to be real than a trick of the light.
26. We also checked every frame for clouds.
