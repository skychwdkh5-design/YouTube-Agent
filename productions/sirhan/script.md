# Wadi As-Sirhan - Ice Age water (Short, 9:16) - DRAFT FOR REVIEW, NOT VOICED

## HOOK
[ON SCREEN: 2015, edge of the fields: green circles against bare sand; WATER FROM THE ICE AGE]
These farms run on water from the last Ice Age.

## PROOF
[ON SCREEN: wipe 2015 -> 1987, same view: empty desert]
In 1987, this was empty desert.
[ON SCREEN: zoom out, timelapse 1987 -> 2000 -> 2008 -> 2015]
Then the circles came.
[ON SCREEN: close-up, individual circles; ~1 KM WIDE; CENTER-PIVOT IRRIGATION]
Each is a farm about a kilometer wide, watered by a rotating sprinkler arm.

## ESCALATION
[ON SCREEN: whole basin, 2000; 1992: 2,000,000+ TONS of wheat exported]
By 1992, the desert kingdom was exporting over two million tons of wheat.

## EXPLANATION
[ON SCREEN: relocate to the northeastern fields; WATER: ~20,000 YEARS OLD]
The water is pumped from deep aquifers filled about twenty thousand years ago.
[ON SCREEN: RAIN: 100-200 MM A YEAR (4-8 in)]
Rain here can't refill them.
[ON SCREEN: core fields, 2015 image; 2016: WHEAT PROGRAM ENDS]
In 2016, Saudi Arabia ended its wheat program to save water.
[ON SCREEN: close-up, 2025 image; ALFALFA - 3x THE WATER of wheat (USDA)]
Many farmers switched to alfalfa, which uses three times the water.

## PAYOFF
[ON SCREEN: widest view, wipe 1987 -> 2025, slow push in; ~50 MORE YEARS (2016 estimate)]
That year, hydrologists estimated about fifty more years of pumping.
All of this, built on water that doesn't come back.
