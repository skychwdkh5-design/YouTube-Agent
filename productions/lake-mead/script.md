# Lake Mead - Everything in orange (Short, 9:16)

## HOOK
[ON SCREEN: 2026 Landsat, orange = water in 2000 now dry, white 2000 shoreline]
Everything in orange was underwater in 2000.

## PROOF
[ON SCREEN: wipe to 2000 full lake, label LAKE MEAD]
Lake Mead. America's largest reservoir.
[ON SCREEN: year flips 2000 2005 2010 2015 2022 2026, same framing]
Watch it drain.

## ESCALATION
[ON SCREEN: label -160 ft / -49 m]
Since 2000, its water level has dropped about 160 feet.
[ON SCREEN: zoom to the northern arm, wipe 2000 -> 2026, label about 9 mi / 14 km]
Its northern arm pulled back about nine miles.
[ON SCREEN: wide, orange fill returns, label ABOUT HALF THE WATER SURFACE GONE]
About half of its surface water is gone.
[ON SCREEN: 2022 frame, label 2022 RECORD LOW]
In 2022, it hit its lowest level since it was first filled in the 1930s.

## EXPLANATION
[ON SCREEN: pan east to the river inflow, pin COLORADO RIVER]
Why? A long drought on the Colorado River.
Year after year, more water left the lake than flowed in.
[ON SCREEN: pull back, label 40 MILLION PEOPLE]
That river system supplies water to about 40 million people.

## PAYOFF
[ON SCREEN: 2026 frame, label AUG 2026 NEW RECORD LOW]
Then, in August 2026, Lake Mead broke that record. Lower than ever.
[ON SCREEN: full lake 2026 with the white 2000 shoreline]
