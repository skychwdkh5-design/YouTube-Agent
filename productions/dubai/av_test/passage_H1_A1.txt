November 2000. A satellite looks down at a patch of the Persian Gulf. There is no island here. Just sea.
November 2003. The same place. A palm tree, fully formed. Between these two pictures: three years.
More than two decades after that first image, an astronaut aboard the International Space Station photographs this coast. The palm-shaped islands stand out from orbit.
So how do you build land where there was only sea? And what happened to the plans that came next?
This is Dubai, in the United Arab Emirates, on the shore of the Persian Gulf. A coastline seems like the one thing on a map that nobody gets to choose. Dubai put that idea to the test. To see how, we go back to the year 2000.
