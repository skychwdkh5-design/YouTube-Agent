# EP002 Dubai — Source Plan

Outline only. Nothing here is a verified claim; claims go to `claims.md`.

## 1. Dubai coastline before major reclamation
- Questions: what the shoreline looked like in the 1980s–90s; Jumeirah, Deira, Creek; population then vs. now.
- Sources to find: Landsat archive (earliest usable scenes), UAE/Dubai government statistics, academic coastal-change papers.
- Imagery: earliest cloud-free Landsat scene of Dubai coast.

## 2. Palm Jumeirah construction
- Questions: start/finish years, volume of sand and rock, dredging method, dimensions, developer.
- Sources: developer and engineering-contractor documents, engineering journals, reputable press.
- Imagery: Landsat time series across construction years.

## 3. The World Islands
- Questions: construction timeline, how much was built, current state, sand and stability issues, ownership.
- Sources: contractor records, peer-reviewed or reputable reporting, recent imagery.
- Imagery: Landsat before/during/after; compare with Palm Jumeirah.

## 4. Engineering and environmental effects
- Questions: dredging and marine habitat, sediment and water circulation, coastal erosion, reef impact, mitigation measures.
- Sources: peer-reviewed studies, environmental assessments, agency reports.
- Keep numeric claims tied to a named study.

## 5. Satellite imagery before/after
- Candidate pairs: coastline (pre-reclamation vs. today), Palm Jumeirah, World Islands, wider Dubai shoreline.
- Tools: /yt-satellite (scene search, metadata), /yt-geo (shared grid), /yt-graphics (annotations).
- Rule: if no suitable scene exists, drop the satellite claim.
