## Channel description (English)
Orbital Atlas explores the strangest places on Earth through real satellite imagery: vanishing lakes, desert farms, remote islands and landscapes reshaped by nature and people. Every video is built on public data (Landsat, NASA) and cites its sources. New episodes on how our planet looks from above, and what the evidence actually says.
