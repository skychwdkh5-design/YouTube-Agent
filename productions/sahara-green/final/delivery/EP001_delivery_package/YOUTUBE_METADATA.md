## Description
The Sahara has turned green more than 230 times in the last 8 million years, according to geological reconstructions - and today, satellites show fields appearing in the middle of the desert. So could we turn the whole Sahara green?

In this video: real Landsat imagery of East Oweinat (Egypt), Kufra (Libya) and Toshka, the orbital cycles behind the "Green Sahara", a 2009 proposal to irrigate the desert with desalinated water, what climate simulations say about rain, dust and storms, and the Great Green Wall.

Note: simulations shown are model results, not forecasts. Figures are quoted from the cited papers.
