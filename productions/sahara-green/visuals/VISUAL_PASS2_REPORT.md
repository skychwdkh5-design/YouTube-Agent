## Changes (all in `scripts/render.py`; pass-1 renderer kept as `scripts/render_v1.py` for comparison)
1. **Text size.** One shared type scale for both sequences (design px at 1080p; 720p = x0.667): kicker 30, date 64 (44 in dissolves), sub-line 32, timeline labels 30, scale labels 30, notes and chip 28, zoom caption 36. Smallest text is now 28 px (18.7 px at 720p; was 20 px, 13 px at 720p), date 64 px (was 46 to 58 px). Font Inter throughout, with a thin dark outline on every glyph.
2. **Toshka timeline and text contrast.** The timeline sits on a compact dark translucent panel (70% black) instead of faint dots on the image. Date block, scale bar and notes got their own small panels, and the heavy full-width top shading was reduced (48% top, 45% bottom gradients, same as before in height). Panels cover small corners only (top-left about 6% of the frame, top-right 4%, two bottom corners). Measured contrast (WCAG ratio of the text colour against the brightest 10% of pixels under each text box, panels included, glyph outline not counted): minimum 5.5:1, all 4.5:1 or better. In pass 1 the same test failed (down to 1.7:1) on sand.
3. **Safe margins.** All text, timeline nodes and scale bars lie inside 96 px at the sides and 72 px top and bottom (5% and 6.7% of the frame); text panels lie inside the 3.5% action-safe area. Bottom captions and the scale bar moved up so they clear the mobile player controls (bottom edge now 72 px from the bottom, was 36 to 62).
4. **Dates stay identifiable in transitions, and no false observation.** During every dissolve the date block lists both dates ("January 2010 / -> January 2016") and both sensors, both timeline nodes turn amber and are joined by an amber segment, and a chip reads "Dissolve, not an observation". Dissolves were shortened from 1.0 and 1.2 s to 0.8 s and use strictly interior blend weights, so no frame shows only one date while mixing two. The East Oweinat left panel text and timeline now fade out together when the zoom starts (pass 1 left the timeline half-clipped at the panel edge during the zoom).
5. **Resolution policy.** Unchanged: Lanczos from the 30 m source, no sharpening, no AI, no synthetic detail; maximum 4.0 screen px per source px at 4K (re-measured). Panels and text are overlays only; they add no image content.
6. **Typography consistency.** Both sequences now use the same code path for the info block (kicker, date, sensor, dissolve chip), the timeline (vertical in East Oweinat, horizontal in Toshka), the scale bar and the notes, with identical fonts, sizes and colours. The earlier East Oweinat title line ("Desert to irrigated circles, 1984 to 2024") was removed so that the two sequences have the same information block; it is not part of the locked narration and can return as a lower third if you want it.
7. **Previews.** `previews/review_package_v2/`: two 720p MP4s (East Oweinat 21.7 s, Toshka 15.9 s) and 10 stills at 1920x1080, including one mid-dissolve frame per sequence.
